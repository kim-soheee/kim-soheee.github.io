He has made everything beautiful in its time.

Ecclesiates 3:11

This verse from the bible is my most favourite and grounding verse. Even when everything around me in busy and chaos, when I fall into a spiral and all I see is the million things out of my control, I trust in God that everything is according to His plan, and that all the trials I may face actually make me grow into a stronger and better person. Everything I do is a step and opportunity to make myself better, and bring up the people around me too. Something something!
